See how algorithms think.

Pick an algorithm, then step through it one move at a time. Every step comes with a plain-English reason for why it happens. Type in your own numbers or draw your own maze.

How it works

1

Pick an algorithm

Choose any card below. The visualizer opens with that algorithm ready to go.

2

Go step by step

Press Next step to move one action at a time, or Play to watch it run. Back rewinds.

3

Understand why

Under the animation, each step says what happened and why the algorithm chose to do it.

4

Make it yours

Enter your own numbers to sort, or draw walls and move the start and end on the grid.

About us

SW

Hi, I'm Swaleha Dalvi, the creator of Stepwise.

I built Stepwise because algorithms are easier to understand when you can watch them think. Textbooks show the final answer. Stepwise shows every comparison, swap and decision along the way, and explains the reasoning behind it.

It is a small, free project written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, made for students, curious beginners and anyone preparing for an interview.

  • 10 algorithms: 6 sorting and 4 pathfinding
  • Step forward and back, with a reason for every move
  • Bring your own data: custom numbers and hand-drawn mazes
